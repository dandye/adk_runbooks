# Runbook Authoring Guidelines

## Objective
Define structural and stylistic requirements for creating clear, agentic security runbooks.

## Inputs
*   `RUNBOOK_TOPIC`: Subject area of the runbook.

## Core Steps

1. Standard Section Order:
   * Include Title, Objective, Inputs, Tools, and numbered Core Workflow Steps.

2. Apply Principle of Maximum Weakness:
   * Write intent-driven outcome directives instead of rigid, brittle procedural trees.

3. Adhere to ASD-STE100 Rules:
   * Use approved controlled vocabulary and keep instructional sentences under 20 words.
